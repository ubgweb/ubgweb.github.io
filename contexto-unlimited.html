How to play Contexto Unlimited

A word game about meaning, not spelling.

1. Guess any word

Type an English word and press Guess. It doesn't have to be related yet — start with a hunch.

2. Read the rank

Each guess gets a rank. #1 means you found the secret word. #500 means your word is in the top 500 closest words in meaning.

3. Move closer

Use the rank and the heat bar to refine your guesses. Lower ranks mean closer meanings. When you hit #1, you win.

Tips for solving Contexto

Start broad. Guess a common word like "house", "water", or "animal". The rank tells you how far you are.

Use the whole family. Once you find a related word, try synonyms, antonyms, and compounds.

Follow the heat. If the bar gets hotter, you're moving in the right direction. If it cools, back up.

Think about context. Contexto is trained on real sentences — the secret word is often used in the same kinds of phrases as related words.

Don't forget plurals, verbs, and adjectives. Trying a different grammatical form can jump you up the list.

Contexto Unlimited FAQ

What is Contexto Unlimited?

It's a word game where you try to guess a hidden word. Each guess is ranked by how close it is in meaning to the answer, so it's about semantics, not spelling.

How does the ranking work?

Ranks are based on how the word is used in millions of sentences. A rank of #1 means you found the secret word. A rank of #100 means your guess is the 100th closest word in meaning.

How many guesses do I get?

As many as you like. You can keep guessing until you find #1, or hit "Give up" to reveal the word and start a new puzzle.

Do I need an account?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser, and your stats are stored only on your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The input and buttons are designed for touch screens and fit small displays. It also works with a keyboard.

Is it affiliated with the original Contexto?

No. Contexto Unlimited is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with the creators of the original Contexto game.

Why didn't my word count?

Only common English words are accepted. If you type something uncommon, a proper noun, or a typo, the game will let you know and won't record a guess.
