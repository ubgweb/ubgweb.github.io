How to play Squaredle Unlimited

A word search where every letter is a stepping stone.

1. Swipe a path

Click or tap letters next to each other (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to form a word. Each cell can only be used once per word.

2. Release to submit

Release the pointer or press Enter to submit. If the word is in the puzzle, it locks in green and scores points.

3. Find every word

The board hides dozens of words of different lengths. Find them all to complete the puzzle and start a new one.

Tips to solve more Squaredle puzzles

Start with short words. Find all the 3-letter words first — they're easier and build momentum.

Look for common prefixes and suffixes. -er, -ing, -ed, re-, un-, and plurals with -s are everywhere.

Trace long paths. Squares with many vowels tend to hide longer words. Explore diagonals.

Use the found letters. Green tiles are already solved; work around them instead of through them.

Clear and try again. If a path dead-ends, hit Clear and start over from a different anchor.

Squaredle Unlimited FAQ

What is Squaredle Unlimited?

It's a word puzzle where you swipe through a square of letters to find every hidden word. Every board is generated from a large English dictionary, and there's no daily limit.

How many puzzles can I play?

As many as you like. Click New puzzle and a fresh board is generated instantly.

How do I submit a word?

Click or tap adjacent letters to form a path. Release the pointer or press Enter to submit. If the word is part of the puzzle, it locks in green.

Do I need an account?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser, and stats are stored only on your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The board uses large touch targets and works in portrait or landscape. It also supports mouse, trackpad, and keyboard.

Is it affiliated with the original Squaredle?

No. Squaredle Unlimited is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with the creators of the original game.

Why didn't my word count?

Only words in our puzzle dictionary are accepted. If a word is valid English but not in the board's list, it won't be found. Try a more common synonym.
