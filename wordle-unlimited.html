How to play Wordle Unlimited

The classic rules, with endless puzzles.

1. Guess a 5-letter word

Type any valid 5-letter English word using your keyboard or the on-screen keys, then press Enter.

2. Read the colors

Green = right letter, right spot. Yellow = right letter, wrong spot. Gray = letter not in the word.

3. Solve in 6 tries

You have six guesses. Solve it, then hit New puzzle to play again right away.

What the colors mean

Each guess tells you something new about the hidden word.

Green

The letter is in the word and in the correct position.

Yellow

The letter is in the word, but in a different position.

Gray

The letter does not appear in the word at all.

Tips for solving Wordle faster

Start with vowel-rich words. Words like "AUDIO", "SLATE" or "CRANE" reveal common letters fast.

Use your second guess to test common letters. Try "PILOT" or "MOUND" if your first guess came up mostly gray.

Watch for double letters. Words like "HAPPY" or "SISSY" have repeats — a gray duplicate means the letter appears only once.

Don't rush the last guess. Narrow the possibilities before you commit.

Use the on-screen keyboard. Keys turn gray, yellow, or green as you learn about letters.

Wordle Unlimited FAQ

What is Wordle Unlimited?

It's the classic Wordle game with no daily limit. Guess a 5-letter word in six tries, then start another puzzle right away.

How many puzzles can I play?

As many as you like. Click New puzzle and a fresh hidden word is chosen instantly.

What counts as a valid guess?

Any 5-letter English word in our dictionary. We use a large list of common words.

Do I need an account?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser, and stats are stored only on your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The board and on-screen keyboard are sized for touch screens and work in portrait or landscape.

Is it affiliated with The New York Times?

No. Wordle Unlimited is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with The New York Times.

Why didn't my word count?

Some valid English words aren't in the answer list or in the guess dictionary. If a word is rejected, try a more common synonym or a different form.
