How to play Spelling Bee Unlimited

Simple rules, endless fun.

1. Make words

Use any of the 7 letters to form words of 4 or more letters. The center letter (yellow) must be in every word.

2. Use letters once

You can use a letter more than once in a word, as long as it's one of the 7 available.

3. Find the pangram

A word that uses all 7 letters is a pangram and scores bonus points. There's at least one in every puzzle.

Tips to find more words

Start with the center letter. Words must include it, so begin there.

Look for common prefixes and suffixes. -ing, -ed, -er, -tion, re-, un-, etc.

Think of plurals. Add -s to words you've already found.

Use the pangram. Try to use all 7 letters — there's always at least one.

Shuffle the letters. Rearranging can reveal hidden words.

Frequently asked questions

What is Spelling Bee Unlimited?

It's a word puzzle where you make words from 7 letters. Every word must contain the center letter. Find the pangram that uses all 7 letters for bonus points.

How many puzzles can I play?

As many as you like. Click "New puzzle" and a fresh set of letters appears instantly.

What is a pangram?

A word that uses all 7 letters at least once. There is always at least one in every puzzle.

Do I need an account?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser. Your stats are stored only on your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The hexagon wheel and input are designed for touch screens, and it also works with a keyboard.

Is it affiliated with The New York Times?

No. Spelling Bee Unlimited is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with The New York Times.

Can I play in other languages?

The word bank is currently English only. More languages coming soon.
