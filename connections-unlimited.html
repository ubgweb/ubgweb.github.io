How to play Connections Unlimited

Each puzzle hides four groups of four words that share something in common.

Select four words

Tap four tiles you think belong together. Tap a selected tile again to remove it.

Press Submit

If you are right, the group is revealed with its category and a color.

Watch your mistakes

You can make three wrong guesses. The fourth ends the puzzle. "One away" means three of your four words are correct.

What the four colors mean

Every puzzle has one group from each difficulty level.

Yellow: easiest

Everyday groups such as fruits, pets or sports.

Green: medium

Specific groups such as cheeses, rivers or card games.

Blue: tricky

Groups built on a shared feature, like things with keys or rings.

Purple: wordplay

Fill-in-the-blank links such as ___ ball or Fire___.

Tips to solve more puzzles

Start with the obvious group. Clearing the easiest set shrinks the board and makes the rest clearer.

Look for red herrings. A word can fit two groups. Count how many words fit each idea before you commit.

Think about parts and features. Words like bridge, keys or rings often link objects that have them.

Try adding a word before or after. If four words all combine with the same one, you have found a wordplay group.

Use Shuffle. A new tile order can reveal connections that were hidden by position.

Connections Unlimited FAQ

What is Connections Unlimited?

It is a word grouping puzzle where you sort 16 words into four hidden categories. Unlike a daily puzzle, you can play as many puzzles as you want.

How many puzzles can I play?

As many as you like. Each puzzle is generated from our word bank, so a new one is always one click away.

How many mistakes are allowed?

You can make three mistakes. The fourth wrong guess ends the puzzle and reveals the answers.

What does "one away" mean?

Three of the four words you picked belong to the same group. Swap one word and try again.

Do I need an account or download?

No. The game runs in your browser and your stats are saved only on your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The board and buttons are sized for phones and tablets, and it also works with a keyboard and mouse.

Is it affiliated with The New York Times?

No. Connections Unlimited is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with The New York Times.
