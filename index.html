Frequently asked questions

What does "unlimited" mean?

Every game lets you start a fresh puzzle whenever you like, so you are not limited to one puzzle a day.

What does "unblocked" mean here?

Games load as ordinary web pages with no downloads, plugins or logins, so they work on most networks. If a school or workplace filters the whole site, we cannot override that.

Do I need an account?

No. Open any game and start playing.

Do the games work on phones?

Yes. Every game is built to fit small screens and touch controls.

Is UBGWeb affiliated with the original games?

No. UBGWeb is an independent fan project. Game names belong to their respective owners.

Is it free?

Yes, every game is free to play.
