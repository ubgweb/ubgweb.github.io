How to play Strands Unlimited

A word search with a twist — every word connects to a hidden theme.

1. Trace a word

Click or tap letters next to each other (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to form a word. Selected letters darken.

2. Submit it

Release or press Enter to submit. If the word is part of the puzzle, it locks in green and the theme is revealed.

3. Find the pangram

One word uses every cell on the grid. Find it to complete the puzzle. Gold tiles mark pangram letters.

What the colors mean

Visual cues help you track what you've found.

Dark tile

The cell is currently selected. Click it again or press Clear to deselect.

Green tile

The cell belongs to a word you've already found.

Gold tile

Part of the pangram — a word that uses every cell on the board.

Light tile

A letter that hasn't been used yet. Try extending your path through it.

Tips to solve more Strands puzzles

Start with the theme. Read the hint above the board — the words all connect to it.

Look for common prefixes and suffixes. Words like -ing, -er, re-, un- often appear in theme lists.

Trace long paths. Short words are easy to find, but the pangram snakes across the board.

Use the found letters. Green tiles are already solved; work around them instead of through them.

Clear and try again. If a path dead-ends, hit Clear selection and start over from a different anchor.

Strands Unlimited FAQ

What is Strands Unlimited?

It's a word search puzzle with a twist: every hidden word belongs to a theme shown above the board. One word, the pangram, uses every cell on the grid.

How many puzzles can I play?

As many as you like. Click New puzzle and a fresh themed board is generated instantly.

How do I submit a word?

Click or tap adjacent letters to form a path. Release or press Enter to submit. If the word is part of the puzzle, it locks in green.

What is a pangram?

A word that uses every single cell on the grid. Every Strands puzzle has exactly one, and finding it completes the puzzle.

Do I need an account?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser, and stats are stored only on your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The board uses large touch targets and works in portrait or landscape. It also supports mouse, trackpad, and keyboard.

Is it affiliated with The New York Times?

No. Strands Unlimited is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with The New York Times.
